## Source and attribution details

For the matched Commons source: **Kirill Tsukanov, "Frog dissection, 5.JPG", 17 March 2012, CC0 1.0 Universal**. A courtesy attribution in the app's credits is appropriate even though CC0 does not require it.

For the muscular teaching image: **Jenna Hellack, "Frog Dissection", Fall 2000**. The digitised copies and slide listings do **not** establish that the image may be redistributed commercially in an Android APK. Either obtain written permission/licence evidence or replace it with a photo created in-house or independently verified as redistributable.

For remaining assets, obtain stable source page, original licence and dimensions, creator permission if applicable, and the exact asset hash before signing production release. The existence of a photograph in a slide sharing site, search result or prior app build does not establish usage rights.

## Academic acceptance is a separate gate

A photograph with acceptable reuse rights may still have incomplete anatomy or misleading hotspots. Visual verification, calibration and Zoology reviewer sign-off must be recorded independently. The Android build passing CI does not establish image rights.

## Release policy

Do not mark the bundled six-plate set "fully copyright cleared" or the application "Play Store ready" while any row is HOLD. Preserve the existing images during the audit and replace only after sourcing suitable recognisable, anatomically correct replacements with verified rights.
